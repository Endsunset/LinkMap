Items

Items are the reusable resources a Project plans with and records during work.

This guide describes the LinkMap iOS app.

On this page
  • Where to Find It
  • What Items Are For
  • Items Stay at the Project Level
  • How Items Become Supplies
  • How Items Become Distributions

Where to Find It

Open a Project detail page, then open Items. Items are managed at the Project level before they are used by Activities.

What Items Are For

Create Items for things the team needs to count, carry, deliver, use, or prepare. Examples include water, kits, food boxes, medicine, tools, radios, printed materials, replacement parts, or inspection equipment.

Items Stay at the Project Level

Items belong to the Project because they are meant to be reused. One Item can appear in many Activities without being recreated. This keeps planning consistent across different work cycles.

How Items Become Supplies

  • Open an Activity and create Assignments for the people or teams doing the work.
  • Add Supplies to an Assignment by choosing from the Project's Items.
  • Set the quantity for each Supply so the Assignment has a clear preparation plan.

How Items Become Distributions

  • During active work, open the Activity Dashboard from the Map.
  • Choose the relevant Stop context.
  • Log a Distribution when an Item is delivered, used, or completed there.
  • Reports can then compare planned Supplies with completed work.
