# How LinkMap Works

A Project keeps your places together. An Activity plans one round of work using those places.

## Start on the Map

Choose a Project to see its places. The iOS app also lets you select an Activity and optional Assignment to focus a round of work. The web map lets you explore the Projects and Locations available to your account.

Build the Project map before planning a Route. When you are ready to work, create an Activity with a Route and Assignments in the iOS app. The same Project and its Locations can be viewed on the web.
