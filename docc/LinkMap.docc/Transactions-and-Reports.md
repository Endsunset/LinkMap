# Transactions and Reports

A Transaction records a positive quantity of one Item moving between resource anchors. Inventory, Activity, Assignment, and Stop are anchors; a balance equals incoming quantities minus outgoing quantities.

## Allowed Movements

Project-level movements connect Outside and Inventory, two Inventories, or Inventory and Activity. Activity-level movements connect Activity, Assignment, and Stop anchors belonging to the same Activity. Item and directional endpoints identify a Transaction; an existing direction must be selected explicitly before editing its quantity or note.

Use the Activity Dashboard for stop work and resource pivots to review balances and history. Assignment reports summarize current routes and net Stop balances; regenerate a report when the underlying work changes.
