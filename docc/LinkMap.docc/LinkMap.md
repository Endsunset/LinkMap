# LinkMap

Plan places, resources, and shared work across iOS and the web.

LinkMap starts with a Project. A Project keeps a reusable map and resources together; Activities organize individual rounds of work. The iOS app creates and manages this structure, while the web map lets you explore available Projects and Locations. [Return to the LinkMap website](https://endsunset.github.io/LinkMap/).
