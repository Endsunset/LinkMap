LinkMap Privacy Policy

  • Last Updated: September 16, 2026
  • Effective Date: May 31, 2025

1. Overview

LinkMap is an iOS and iPadOS app for organizing location-based community work.

We do not collect your personal data for advertising, profiling, or sale, and we do not operate separate servers that store your LinkMap Project data. Information you create in LinkMap is stored using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services so the app can save, synchronize, and share it when you choose to collaborate.

2. Data Stored in LinkMap

LinkMap may store information you create, such as Project names, map locations and areas, Activities, Routes, Stops, Assignments, Items, Supplies, Distributions, reports, and photos.

This information is used only to provide LinkMap's features. Project data is stored in your private iCloud CloudKit database by default.

Photos attached through LinkMap are stored as private user data in CloudKit.

3. Project Sharing

You can choose to share a Project with other people using Apple CloudKit sharing. People who accept the share may be able to view or modify shared Project information according to the permissions of the share.

Ending collaboration removes future access through CloudKit, but it cannot remove information that another person has already copied or saved outside LinkMap.

4. Location and Maps

LinkMap uses Apple MapKit and may request access to your device location for map-related features. LinkMap does not require continuous background location tracking.

Map display, search, directions, and related requests may be processed directly by Apple. We do not receive your device location for our own use. You can change LinkMap's location permission at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Locations saved inside a Project are user-created Project data and are not necessarily your device's current location.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

LinkMap does not contain third-party advertising trackers, does not sell your personal information, and does not use your Project or Activity data for advertising or profiling.

Apple may provide the developer with crash reports, diagnostics, or App Store analytics according to Apple's services, privacy policies, and your device settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

LinkMap data stored in CloudKit remains there until it is deleted by you, by another user with permission, or according to Apple's iCloud policies.

Deleting LinkMap from a device does not necessarily delete data already stored in iCloud. Project owners can delete their data or end Project collaboration in LinkMap, and collaborators can leave shared Projects. You can also manage applicable iCloud data and permissions through your Apple Account and device settings.

7. Apple Services

LinkMap relies on Apple services, including iCloud, CloudKit, MapKit, and system location services. Information processed by Apple is handled according to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies and may be processed in locations where Apple operates.

8. Children's Privacy

LinkMap is not directed to children under 13. Do not use LinkMap to store personal information about children unless you have an appropriate reason and authority to do so.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when LinkMap's features or data practices change. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App Store listing or another appropriate channel.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the developer through the LinkMap GitHub repository. Do not include private or sensitive information in a public GitHub issue:

https://github.com/Endsunset/LinkMap/issues
